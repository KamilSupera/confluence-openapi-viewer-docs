# Privacy policy — OpenAPI (Swagger) Viewer for Confluence

The app runs entirely on Atlassian Forge. It has no servers of its own.

- Macro settings (spec URL or attachment name, display options, optional credentials) are stored in the Confluence page as macro configuration, by Atlassian, under your site's data residency.
- The definition is fetched by the reader's browser directly from the URL you configure, or from the page attachment through the Confluence API. The app does not store, cache, log or transmit it anywhere else.
- No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party services. The optional online validator is off by default; if you enable it, your browser sends the definition URL to the validator you configure.
